pantry-sync
Keep the pantry — the product's source of truth — honest, at a friction cost a tired human will actually pay. This feature carries the core bet.
STATUS: IN-REVIEW — 2026-06-02 1 open question: P7 barcode scope depends on mealmate-prd.md §Features contracts consumed by shopping-list-gen

01The loop: census once, sync forever

Two jobs: the one-time census (bounded, one sitting) and the recurring sync — counts change as a side effect of living (shopping, cooking), never as a chore. Corrections repair drift; events explain it.

census
one guided sitting; name + unit + count; ≤15 min for 40 items
→
shop-receipt event
confirm the list into the pantry — ≤3 taps for 25 lines
→
meal-cooked event
planned or improvised; decrements ingredient lines
→
audit + undo
90-day event log; undo = compensating correction, history never rewritten

04Things to poke at

One item stands between this PRD and settled.

P7 — Barcode scanning: accelerator inside shop-receipt, or v2 entirely?
For: 3/12 interviewees said scanning is the only way they'd keep counts current, and shop-receipt confirmation is where taps concentrate. Against: coverage is worst exactly where waste happens (produce/bulk), and a camera + product-DB dependency cuts against the lean local-first PWA. What changes with the answer: the shop-receipt flow's contract, the permission surface, and whether a product-name lookup dependency enters v1.
