mealmate
Plan a week of meals in five minutes, shop once, waste nothing — by making the pantry first-class and deriving everything else from it.
STATUS: SETTLED — 2026-05-30 depends on mealmate-brief.md (settled 2026-05-26) evidence: 12 user interviews (research/) detail: mealmate-prd.md

01The shape: one hub, three features

Local-first PWA, all data on-device in v1 — no accounts, no server. The pantry is the source of truth; the plan draws on it, the shopping list is computed as plan-minus-pantry, and cooking updates it so next week starts true.

PANTRY — the hub
counted stock per unit · kept honest by pantry-sync's events (shop-receipt, meal-cooked)
pantry-sync in-review
census + event-driven updates. Carries the core bet.
shopping-list-gen next
plan-minus-pantry, aggregated by unit. Consumes pantry contracts.
week-planner later
day × meal grid; the demand side of the list.

04Things to poke at

The round-1 agenda, kept with resolutions for the record — this PRD is settled. The one live question in the whole planning space now sits in the pantry-sync feature PRD (P7, barcode scope).

Should the first shipped feature be the visible one (shopping list) instead of pantry-sync?
Resolved in round 1 → no (R5): a list derived from an untrusted pantry is worse than no list; prove the pantry loop first.
Is on-device-only too austere even for v1?
Resolved in round 1 → local-first stands (R6): no account wall before the core bet is proven; export/backup is a pantry-sync detail.
